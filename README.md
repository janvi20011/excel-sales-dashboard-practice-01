# Excel Sales Dashboard – Practice Project 01

## Overview

This project is an interactive Sales Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel as part of my Data Analytics learning and practice.

The dashboard focuses on analyzing sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product preferences, delivery time, customer location, and customer satisfaction.

This project helped me practice Excel-based data analysis, dashboard design, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, KPIs, and data visualization.

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to practice transforming sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

The dashboard was designed to analyze:

- Sales performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product popularity
- Customer demographics
- Delivery time
- Customer satisfaction
- Geographic distribution of customers

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Last 13 Weeks Trends

A trend analysis of quantity and sales amount over the last 13 weeks.

This helps understand changes in sales performance and order quantity over time.

### 2. Customer Purchase Channels

The dashboard analyzes how customers prefer to purchase products through different channels:

- App
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

The analysis is also segmented by gender.

### 3. Customer Purchase Quantity

This section shows how many products customers purchase per order.

It helps understand customer buying patterns and order size.

### 4. Product Popularity by Gender

The dashboard compares product popularity across different gender categories.

Products include:

- T-Shirts
- Jeans
- Sneakers
- Tank Tops
- Bikinis
- Shorts
- Sundresses
- Graphic Tees
- Hoodies & Sweatshirts
- Sandals
- Crop Tops
- Leggings
- Casual Dresses
- Workout Tops
- Pajama Sets
- Maxi Dresses
- Sunglasses
- Tote Bags
- Baseball Caps
- Jewelry

### 5. Customer Geographic Distribution

A map visualization is used to show the geographic distribution of customers and sales amount.

### 6. Shipping and Delivery Time

This visualization analyzes the number of days taken to deliver orders.

### 7. Customer Satisfaction

Customer ratings from 1 to 5 are analyzed across different months to understand customer satisfaction patterns.
